## Key Points and takeaways
*   Kyle Fowler built two successful apps, Cardstock and Scanemon, generating over $120,000/month by identifying and solving personal problems related to his hobbies (sports and Pokémon card collecting).

*   Cardstock, a sports card value scanner, launched 6 years ago and generates $75k MRR from 15,000 subscriptions. Scanemon, for Pokémon cards, launched a year ago and already has $17k MRR from 3,400 subscriptions, with more new customers monthly than Cardstock.

## Topics Covered
*   **Kyle Fowler's Background and App Success:** Kyle Fowler, an iOS developer, turned his passion for card collecting into a career. His first app, Cardstock, a sports card value scanner, was built over six months and now makes $75,000 MRR. His second app, Scanemon, for Pokémon cards, was built in just one day using AI and quickly achieved $17,000 MRR.
*   **The "Problem-Solving" Approach to Idea Generation:** Kyle's core philosophy is to find app ideas by looking at his own hobbies and personal frustrations. He believes that if you have a problem, others likely do too, making it a viable business opportunity. He encourages documenting daily "slow arduous tasks" as potential app ideas.
*   **App Development with AI:** Kyle highlights the transformative role of AI in app development. While Cardstock was built using traditional methods (Swift, SwiftUI, Stack Overflow, AWS), Scanemon's Minimum Viable Product (MVP) was generated by Cursor's AI agent in a single day. He suggests AI can now handle much of the build process, including setting up monetization tools like Superwall or RevenueCat.
*   **Tech Stack and Tools:** The apps primarily use Swift and SwiftUI for the frontend, Core Data for local storage, and AWS EC2 for the backend database. Other tools in their tech stack include RevenueCat for subscriptions, and a range of AI and productivity tools such as Codex (ChatGPT), Notion, Cursor, Slack, Framer, Claude, Supabase, Railway, Code Rabbit, WhisperFlow, and Granola.
*   **Marketing Strategy for 2026:** For marketing, Kyle strongly recommends TikTok slideshows due to their simplicity and repeatability. He advises finding a balance between views and conversions. He also suggests using platforms like Noise to distribute these slideshows to creators, paying based on views, or starting with personal TikTok accounts to validate content.
*   **Advice for Aspiring Builders:** Kyle's main advice is to not be afraid to seek help and information from successful individuals. He believes people who have achieved success are often eager to share their journey and insights, making it easier for newcomers to learn and grow. He also emphasizes the current opportunity to build and test ideas rapidly due to AI advancements.
