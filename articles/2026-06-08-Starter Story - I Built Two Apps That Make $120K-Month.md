## Overview
Kyle Fowler has built two highly successful apps, Cardstock and Scanon, generating over $120,000 a month by identifying and solving personal problems related to his hobbies. He advocates for a straightforward approach to app development, emphasizing finding ideas in plain sight, building minimal viable solutions rapidly with AI, and leveraging simple, repeatable marketing strategies. His journey demonstrates that passion-driven problem-solving can lead to significant financial success.

## Key Points
-   Kyle Fowler built two apps, Cardstock (sports cards) and Scanon (Pokemon cards), making over $120K/month ($1.5M/year).
-   His core method for finding app ideas is to identify personal problems or tedious tasks within his own hobbies and passions.
-   Cardstock is a sports card value scanner ($75K MRR, 15K active subscriptions), and Scanon is a Pokemon card value scanner ($17K MRR, 3.4K active subscriptions).
-   He emphasizes building a minimal viable solution quickly, leveraging AI tools like Cursor and ChatGPT for rapid development (Scanon's MVP was made in one day).
-   For marketing in 2026, he recommends simple, repeatable TikTok slideshows, finding what converts and gets views, and using platforms like Noise for creator distribution.
-   His advice includes not overthinking the process, just building, and recognizing that people are usually happy to help and share information.

## Topics Covered
**Origin of App Ideas:** Kyle explains that his app ideas came directly from his personal hobbies and problems. As a lifelong card collector, he noticed a lack of effective tools to catalog, sort, and value his collection, leading him to build Cardstock. He stresses that the best app ideas often hide in plain sight within one's own daily life and passions.

**App Functionality and Revenue:** The video showcases Cardstock, a sports card value scanner that uses a phone's camera to identify cards, display market values, track collections, and show recent sales data. It currently generates approximately $75,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR) from 15,000 active subscriptions. Scanon, a similar app for Pokemon cards launched a year ago, is already achieving $17,000 MRR from 3,400 active subscriptions, demonstrating rapid growth.

**Building Process and Tech Stack:** Kyle details the development of Cardstock, which began in 2019 using Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, and an AWS EC2 backend, largely self-taught via Stack Overflow. For Scanon, built in 2025, he heavily utilized AI tools like Cursor and ChatGPT, enabling him to create the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) in just one day. The broader tech stack includes RevenueCat and Superwall for subscriptions, Notion, Slack, Framer, Claude, Superbase, Railway, Code Rabbit, Whisperflow, and Granola.

**Playbook for Finding and Marketing Apps in 2026:** Kyle provides a step-by-step guide: 1) Identify problems or opportunities by noting down repetitive or arduous tasks in your life. 2) Determine the most minimal solution to solve that problem. 3) Build it, highlighting that AI has made app development significantly easier and faster. For marketing, he suggests focusing on simple, repeatable TikTok slideshows, finding formats that convert and get views, and using platforms like Noise to distribute content through creators or starting with your own TikTok accounts.

**Advice for Aspiring Builders:** Kyle's key advice is to build for something you're passionate about and that solves your own problems, ensuring a win regardless of monetary success. He encourages people not to overthink the process, to just get started, and to not be afraid to ask for help or information, as successful individuals are often willing to share their knowledge.

## Key Takeaways
-   **Personal Problems are Goldmines:** Your own hobbies and daily frustrations are the most authentic and often overlooked sources for valuable app ideas.
-   **AI Accelerates Everything:** Leverage modern AI tools to drastically reduce development time, allowing you to ship MVPs in hours or days and quickly test market viability.
-   **Simplicity in Marketing Wins:** Focus on simple, repeatable content formats like TikTok slideshows to efficiently drive app downloads and build an audience.
-   **Don't Overthink, Just Build:** The biggest hurdle is often analysis paralysis. The current environment allows for rapid building and testing, so prioritize action over endless planning.
-   **Embrace Community and Learning:** Don't hesitate to seek advice or information from others; successful people are often eager to share their insights and help.
