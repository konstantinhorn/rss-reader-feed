## Context
This video provides a detailed review and test of the Midea Porter Split mobile air conditioner, focusing on its performance, power consumption, noise levels, and usability in a rental apartment.

## Key Points and Takeaways
*   **Effective Cooling:** The Midea Porter Split significantly cools rooms quickly (e.g., 4°C in 40 minutes for a 15m² room), making living spaces comfortable even in extreme heat.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** As a split unit, it's more efficient than monoblock ACs, consuming 250-850W on average, up to 1200W initially, but settling around 800W. Maintaining temperature uses 150-350W in Silent mode.
*   **Noise Level:** While powerful, the unit is generally too loud for sleeping, especially the outdoor unit in Silent mode (50 dB) and the irregular condensate pumping sound. Pre-cooling a room and then using a quiet fan for the night is recommended.
*   **Installation & Portability:** Designed for rental apartments, it's relatively easy to install at windows or balconies using included accessories, without damaging the window. It weighs 45kg, but the outdoor unit is only 10kg.
*   **Multi-functional:** It can cool, heat (untested in video), and dehumidify effectively (e.g., 10% humidity reduction in an hour).
*   **Smart Features:** Controllable via remote (with iSense function as an external thermostat) and a comprehensive app (Midea Air), offering detailed settings, sleep mode, Eco mode, and energy monitoring. Home Assistant integration is possible via a custom component.
*   **Limitations:** The 2m hose and 1.8m power cable limit placement. The open window during operation poses a security risk. Cooling multiple, larger, or convoluted rooms effectively with one unit is challenging.

## Topics Covered
*   **Pre-Air Conditioner Measures:** Before using the AC, the reviewer tried insulating pleats (insufficient) and UV film on the outside of windows. The UV film significantly reduced heat buildup, suggesting it can save energy for the AC.
*   **Midea Porter Split Overview:** The unit weighs 45 kg and comes with indoor/outdoor units, remote, window bracket, and sealing. It's larger than it appears in videos but has a sturdy design. Split units are more efficient than monoblocks, prevent negative pressure, and are suitable for apartments with gas meters.
*   **Installation Process:** Installation involves attaching a window sealing fabric with Velcro to the window frame and using a bracket for the outdoor unit (10kg, IPX4 certified). The outdoor unit is secured with a steel cable. The flat hose allows the window to close to 7-8 cm. Window sealing is recommended for efficiency. Operating with an open window poses a security risk, and the outdoor unit can be removed in about 2 minutes.
*   **Functions and Controls:** The Porter Split can cool, heat, and dehumidify. Settings are available on the unit, via remote (with iSense function as an external thermostat), or the Midea Air app. The app offers fine-tuned fan speed, a turbo mode, sleep mode (customizable temperature curve), an AI-powered Eco mode for efficiency, and an "Ion function" to reduce odors.
*   **Cooling Performance Test:** A 15m² room cooled from 26.7°C to 22.7°C in 40 minutes (4°C drop). Reaching 21°C took over 5 hours as the AC regulated down prematurely due to local temperature readings. In Turbo mode, it initially drew 1200W (at 35°C ambient), settling at 800W after an hour. Daily consumption was 4-5.8 kWh. To maintain temperature, it uses 150-350W in Silent mode. Cooling multiple, larger, or convoluted rooms effectively with one unit was largely ineffective. After cooling, the room temperature rose by 2°C in one hour and 4°C in five hours. Continuous operation on hot days is recommended.
*   **Noise Levels:** In Turbo mode, the indoor unit measured 56 dB and the outdoor unit 60 dB. In Silent mode (with 1% fan speed via app), the indoor unit was 47 dB and the outdoor unit 50 dB. The outdoor unit is noticeably louder than the indoor unit in Silent mode. An irregular condensate pumping noise occurs approximately every 30 minutes, which can be disruptive for sleep.
*   **Dehumidifying Performance:** In a 21m² living room, humidity dropped from 58% to 48% in one hour, consuming about 240W.
*   **Smart Home Integration:** The Midea Air app allows remote control and energy monitoring. Home Assistant integration is possible via the "Midea AC LAN" custom component (cloud-based, local control possible with a separate stick).
*   **Price and Availability:** The unit is currently hard to find, with prices varying significantly (e.g., €780 at hardware stores vs. €1200 online). Early purchase is advised.
*   **Conclusion:** The Midea Porter Split is effective for cooling and improving comfort in hot weather. Installation is straightforward, and electricity costs are manageable. However, its noise level, particularly the outdoor unit and condensate pumping, makes it unsuitable for sleeping with it running. Minor improvements like a temperature offset, quieter sleep mode, and a longer, replaceable power cable would enhance the product.
